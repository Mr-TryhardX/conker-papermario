Preserve the entire original Hybrid Heaven campaign, story, levels, exploration, collision, enemies and progression, with No Mercy wrestlers and wrestling combat. Deliver Windows builds for the user to play. The separate standalone facility remains an earlier prototype.

- Full 3D wrestlers, including Kane, with original attire choices and imported animations, paired grapples and finishers.
- Individual wrestler stats and perks, leveling, upgrades, earned currency, items and purchasable moves, saved character and campaign progression.
- No Mercy navigation/menu style and shop music.
- Replace the circular field health display with a No Mercy spirit-style meter, driven by actual Hybrid Heaven current/max health. Color/fill must respond to damage and healing. This repurposes the spirit meter as health; separate spirit/finisher mechanics remain independent. Native HUD drawing and health-source identification are pending, so the current preview still shows the original gauge. User priority: implement this last, after core combat and campaign work.
- No Mercy music during combat; retain the earlier request for random wrestler themes when battles begin where appropriate.
- Audience cheers when a move lands, along with original impact/voice effects. Identify and validate actual crowd samples before naming bank indices as cheers.
- Full No Mercy-style Create-a-Wrestler: appearance and clothing customization, multiple attire slots, moveset editing, stats/perks, and saved custom characters. This is added scope, not an already implemented feature. Imported appearances and move IDs must be checked against the original format rather than silently reduced to the six-player prototype.

The next playable milestone is one original Hybrid Heaven level using a complete animated Kane, connected combat, music/SFX, progression and saving. Full campaign completion is measured by playable story/level validation, not extracted asset counts. No deadline or campaign percentage is guaranteed.
